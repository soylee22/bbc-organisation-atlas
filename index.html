Operating organisation

Director-General & ExCo

The DG is chief executive and editor-in-chief. ExCo manages services and delivery under the Board’s strategy.

Matt Brittin became DG on 18 May 2026. The Deputy DG role was announced in June. The Group COO role closed on 1 September 2026.

[2] p. 84
  • Division / commissioning

    Content

    Television, iPlayer, sport, children’s and education. Commissions programmes from BBC Studios and independent producers.

    Channels and editorial teams
    • BBC One, Two, Three and Four. iPlayer brings live and on-demand television together.
    • Drama, comedy, entertainment, factual, arts, religion and specialist commissioning.
    • BBC Sport, including television, radio and digital coverage with other BBC teams.
    • Children’s & Education: CBBC, CBeebies, Bitesize, teacher resources and learning campaigns.
    • Commissioning, channel scheduling and audience services are distinct from Studios’ commercial production units.
    [2] creative remit and service data
  • Operating area within Content

    Audio / radio

    Public radio networks, BBC Sounds, music, speech and live events. Radio commissioning is distinct from Studios Audio’s production business.

    Networks and audio services
    • Radio 1, 1Xtra, Radio 2, Radio 3, Radio 4, Radio 4 Extra, Radio 5 Live, 5 Sports Extra, 6 Music and Asian Network.
    • Additional music stations include Radio 1 Dance, Radio 1 Anthems and Radio 3 Unwind.
    • BBC Sounds provides UK live radio, on-demand programmes, podcasts and music discovery.
    • News supplies journalism to radio. Nations supplies local and national services.
    • BBC orchestras, choirs and the Proms form part of the public cultural offer.
    [2] audio and service data
  • Division / journalism

    News & Current Affairs

    UK and international journalism across television, radio and digital. Includes BBC World Service.

    News and World Service
    • National television bulletins, BBC News channel, BBC Parliament, investigations and current affairs.
    • News website/app, live reporting, verification and radio news programmes.
    • World Service English and language services: public-service international journalism funded by licence fee and FCDO grant.
    • International BBC News commercial distribution and BBC.com monetisation sit with Studios. Editorial journalism remains governed by BBC standards.
    [2] pp. 18-27, 60, 74-79
  • Division / place-based services

    Nations

    England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. National, regional and local services connect the BBC to communities.

    National and local services
    • England: regional television news, local radio and local digital reporting.
    • Scotland: BBC Scotland channel, Radio Scotland and Radio nan Gàidheal. BBC ALBA operates in partnership with MG ALBA.
    • Wales: BBC Radio Wales, Radio Cymru and Radio Cymru 2, plus Welsh television and digital output. S4C is a separate broadcaster.
    • Northern Ireland: BBC Radio Ulster, Radio Foyle and local television/digital services.
    • Nations’ production and commissioning work crosses Content, News and Audio. The four Nations Committees advise the Board.
    [2] pp. 48-55, 120-121, 225 onwards
  • Group-wide capability

    BBC Media Tech

    Product, technology and engineering across the BBC Group. Announced in March 2026 to bring teams together.

    Products and infrastructure
    • Product design and software for iPlayer, Sounds, News, Sport, accounts and personalisation.
    • Broadcast technology, playout, distribution, cyber security, data and research & development.
    • The Chief Technology and Product Officer leads this capability. It supports public and commercial operations.
    • Products are shared services and audience brands, not separate corporate parents.
    [2] pp. 59, 63, 84
  • Corporate support

    Strategy, audiences & operations

    Finance, people, customer/audience insight, marketing, corporate affairs, strategy and transformation enable the whole group.

    Support and related interests
    • Legal, editorial policy and complaints, procurement, property, safety, accessibility and business support.
    • Selected directly held service companies include BBC Property, BBC Media Applications Technologies and news/World Service entities.
    • Distribution associates: Everyone TV 25% and YouView 16.7% at 31 March 2026.
    • BBC Media Action and Children in Need have their own charity governance. The accounts assess them as associates without entitlement to a share of results.
    [2] pp. 84, 199-201
  • Related bodies · mixed relationships

    Platforms, partnerships & charities

    Separate ownership, funding, partnership and trustee relationships sit alongside the operating divisions.

    Related organisations and boundaries
    • Everyone TV, 25% BBC interest: shared platform operator for Freeview, Freesat and Freely. YouView is a separate 16.7% associate.
    • BBC ALBA ↔ MG ALBA: joint service partnership, not BBC ownership of MG ALBA.
    • S4C: separate Welsh-language broadcaster. BBC supplies programmes and licence-fee funding. £98m reported for S4C obligations in 2025/26.
    • BBC Media Action / Children in Need: separately governed charities, assessed as associates without BBC entitlement to results. Media Action is distinct from World Service.
    • BBC Pension Trust Limited: trustee company for the pension scheme. The annual report lists it as directly held, but Companies House records the BBC controller ceased on 1 October 2025. Current control is unresolved. Scheme assets are for members.
    • BBC News Trading Limited, no. 08101280: directly held company, formerly BBC World Service Trading Limited, renamed on 18 June 2026. This company is distinct from the World Service editorial service.

BBC Commercial Limited

The commercial holding company. Formerly BBC Commercial Holdings Limited, renamed on 1 April 2022. This is one company.

Commercial governance

The Commercial Board oversees commercial ambitions and reports to the BBC Board. The Studios Executive Committee manages the Studios group under delegated authority.

    BBC Studios Limited

    The main commercial group. Creates, invests in, distributes and monetises content for the BBC and other customers.

    [2] pp. 74-79, 82, 202-206[5] Annual review
    • 100% subsidiary · no. 09463829

      BBC Studios Productions Limited

      Production company supporting the units and labels mapped below.

    • 100% subsidiary · no. 01420028

      BBC Studios Distribution Limited

      Global sales, rights and investment. Owns UKTV Media Holdings Limited, which owns UKTV Media Limited.

      [2] Company schedule[12] Legal chain
    • Business segment · operating structure

      Content Studio

      Production, content investment, worldwide distribution, formats and brand exploitation. Global Content brings development, production and sales together.

      Production units and group companies
      • BBC Studios Productions Limited and production subsidiaries support UK programme-making.
      • Scripted units: Drama, Comedy and Fiction.
      • Unscripted units: Natural History, Science, Documentary, Specialist Factual, Entertainment, Events and Music.
      • Kids & Family: production, development and franchise management. CBBC and CBeebies channels remain public services.
      • BBC Studios Audio: radio and podcast production for BBC commissioners and commercial platforms, plus international audio distribution.
      • Global Productions: nine bases across the US, Australia, France, Germany, Spain, Benelux, Norway, Sweden and Denmark. The wider group also has Indian and African production companies.
      • Global Content Sales: investment, co-production and worldwide sales teams working across the four genres.
      • Other wholly owned production groups: Brutal Media (Spain), Werner Film (Australia) and Rapid Blue (South Africa). Selected entities are shown, not every local subsidiary.
      Controlled labels and minority investments

      Group-held stakes at 31 March 2026. An operating label can contain several legal entities. Minority investments do not imply full control.

      • 100%: Clerkenwell Films, House Productions, Lookout Point, Sid Gentle Films, Firebird Pictures, Voltage TV and Mothership Productions.
      • Baby Cow: majority-owned, 75% last reported. The 2025/26 accounts say 75%, while the current Global Content page calls it fully acquired. The current stake is unresolved.
      • 40% investment: Boffola Pictures. The group schedule and current portfolio use different classifications. Accounting control is unresolved.
      • 23%: Expectation Entertainment. 25%: Mettlemouse Entertainment, Samphire Films and Various Artists. 20%: Turbine Studios.
      • Moonage Pictures: exited. ITV announced its acquisition on 1 April 2025. Continuing distribution rights do not imply BBC equity.
      • Commercial distribution or development partnerships with producers such as John Downer, Mindhouse, SISTER and Zinc do not establish BBC ownership.
      • Bluey is made by Ludo Studio. Studios’ distribution, licensing and franchise role does not mean it owns Ludo.
      Distribution, licensing, products and music
      • BBC Studios Distribution Limited: global programme and format sales, licensing and investment in BBC and independent content.
      • Sales to broadcasters and streaming platforms, co-production finance, pre-sales and rights investment help fund programmes.
      • Consumer products, publishing licences, games, live events and experiences exploit franchises such as Bluey, Doctor Who and BBC Earth.
      • Demon Music Group and MCI Music Publishing sit within the commercial group. Music rights businesses differ from public BBC orchestras and Studios Music production.
      • 2Entertain entities support home-entertainment rights. Ownership and contractual rights vary by title and territory.
      [2] pp. 74-79, 202-206[6] Production portfolio[7] Audio
    • Business segment · operating structure

      Media & Streaming

      Commercial channels, digital services, advertising and subscriptions. Includes UKTV, BritBox International and international BBC media.

      UKTV / U
      • Legal chain: Studios → Distribution → UKTV Media Holdings Limited → UKTV Media Limited. These are wholly owned group companies. UKTV commissions, acquires and schedules content from multiple producers.
      • U: free streaming service and umbrella brand.
      • Free channels: U&Dave, U&DRAMA, U&W, U&YESTERDAY and U&eden.
      • Paid channels: U&GOLD and U&alibi.
      • FAST channels and platform partnerships provide further distribution. Services and availability vary by territory.
      BritBox International and streaming
      • BritBox International Limited and related entities are wholly owned. BBC Studios bought ITV’s remaining 50% in March 2024.
      • International subscription streaming for British television. Distinct from the former BritBox UK service integrated into ITVX.
      • BBC Select: factual subscription streaming. BBC Player: branded on-demand service distributed through partners.
      • Digital channels, social video, FAST services and partnerships extend the commercial portfolio.
      BBC.com, international news, channels and audio
      • BBC.com / BBC app: international digital news and features, video and audio. Advertising and paid offers support commercial income.
      • BBC News international channel: commercial distribution and advertising sit with Studios. Editorial news is produced within the BBC’s journalism framework.
      • BBC Global News Limited, BBC.com Limited and BBC World Distribution Limited: selected group companies supporting these operations.
      • International channels include BBC Earth, BBC First, BBC Brit and CBeebies, with locally tailored brands and partner arrangements.
      • International audio carries BBC podcasts and radio through BBC.com and commercial/platform distribution. Public World Service remains part of News.
      • BBC America is outside BBC ownership: AMC bought Studios’ remaining 50.1% on 1 November 2024. Brand/content relationships do not create a BBC stake.
      [2] pp. 74-79, 203-206[8] UKTV channels[9] BritBox purchase[10] AMC filing
  • Wholly owned subsidiary · no. 03593793

    BBC Studioworks Limited

    A sibling of Studios under BBC Commercial. Sells studio facilities, technical services and post-production to BBC and external producers.

    Facilities and services
    • Television Centre, London: TC1, TC2 and TC3.
    • Elstree Film Studios: George Lucas 1 and 2.
    • Kelvin Hall, Glasgow: television studio and production facilities.
    • Studio engineering, equipment, crews, editing, grading, audio and digital post-production.
    • Facility hire supports production. It does not make Studioworks the commissioner or owner of every programme filmed there.

How to read this map

This atlas combines a company ownership tree with an operating map. Dashed branches group activities and brands, not legal subsidiaries or exact staff reporting lines.

Coverage: the main organisation and selected significant businesses. It omits many production SPVs, dormant entities and local subsidiaries. Ownership stakes use the 31 March 2026 accounts, with later primary updates checked to 4 October 2026.

Limits and interpretation

Operating groupings simplify a matrix organisation. They do not assert that every listed service reports through one manager. Group-held stakes may be indirect.

Baby Cow’s current equity percentage remains unresolved. Boffola and other minority investments are shown with stakes, without inferring accounting control. Pension Trust control also needs reconciliation.

The annual report’s ExCo membership is dated 29 June 2026. This atlas incorporates its stated September COO closure and avoids presenting that list as a current personnel chart.

BBC access restrictions prevented direct retrieval of some live BBC pages. The downloaded government-hosted 2025/26 report anchors those claims. This is the main organisation, not a register of every SPV or contractual rights interest.
